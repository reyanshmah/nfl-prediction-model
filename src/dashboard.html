A different market than everything above — against the spread (ATS), not moneyline. Only applies to games where the market already has a big favorite (|market_spread| ≥ 7 points); every other game is excluded, not just ranked low. Among those, bets the side the model's predicted margin disagrees with the spread on: if the model thinks the favorite wins by less than the spread says (or the underdog wins outright), it bets the underdog to cover; if the model thinks the favorite wins by even more than the spread says, it bets the favorite to cover. Ranked by the size of that disagreement: edge = |model_margin − market_spread|, priced with the real spread odds (home_spread_odds / away_spread_odds, usually near −110).

Backtested on real closing lines and real results, held out both times (never trained on), each with a fair 3-season training window: betting every qualifying game, no ranking needed — +8.8% ROI in 2025 (72 bets), +25.4% ROI in 2024 (52 bets), both independently profitable — the single best result out of every sort, combination, and pattern tested against this project's data. Note the 2025 number specifically dropped a lot (was +19.5%) once 2021 data was added to fix incomplete rolling stats for early-2022 games, a reminder that even a "survived two seasons" result can still move once the underlying data improves.
The best-performing moneyline sort found — better than Combined, Highest Agreement, or any single sort on its own. Only bets a game when THREE separate signals all point the same way: Market Confidence, Highest Agreement (model and market pick the same winner), and Hidden Edge (model confident while the market itself is unsure) all have to agree — which, once the model and market already agree on who wins at all, happens automatically. Among those games, ranks by the average of each signal's own z-score that week (so a 0-1 probability and a raw edge number are on comparable footing before combining), and bets the shared side at its real moneyline.

Backtested +8.3% ROI in 2025, +3.2% ROI in 2024 (top 5 picks/week, real held-out seasons, real odds, fair 3-season training window both times) — positive in both, and higher than every individual sort tested on its own.
